The left sidebar of a project is grouped into four sections. This page lists each item under its sidebar label, with the page that covers it. Items marked **No page yet** have no dedicated documentation page today.

## What the sidebar shows

The sidebar starts with the **PROJECT** box, where you pick the project, and a **Project Home** link. Below them come the four sections, and **Manage Project** closes the list.

- A section appears only if your project role lets you see at least one of its pages. A page you cannot see is left out.
- Under **Data & AI Assets**, five items are folded away. Click **Show all assets** to reveal **Models**, **Skills / Prompts**, **RAGs / Knowledge Bases**, **Code Repos** and **Global Functions**, and **Show less** to fold them again.
- Use the arrow button next to the logo to collapse the sidebar to icons. On a narrow screen, the menu button in the header opens it as a drawer.
- **Learning Hub** appears at the bottom of the sidebar when it is turned on for your installation.
- A **Review-only user** sees a reduced menu: **My assignments**, **Feedback Portals** and **Annotation Queues**, plus **Open the full platform** when their access allows it.
