[Deepgram](https://deepgram.com/) is a voice AI platform with APIs for speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and full speech-to-speech voice agents. GGX can use it as a voice provider.

## What it does in GGX

- Make Deepgram's speech and voice agent capabilities available to the organization once it is connected.

## How to connect

1. In GGX, open **Org Settings → Integrations → Voice providers** and select **Deepgram**.
2. Enter your Deepgram API key and save.

## Notes

- A project can use its own Deepgram key instead of the organization's.
