[Weights & Biases](https://wandb.ai/) provides experiment tracking, monitoring, and observability for AI workloads. GGX can connect it alongside your other observability tools.

## What it does in GGX

- Make your Weights & Biases account available to the organization, so users can work with W&B from GGX workflows.

## How to connect

1. In GGX, open **Org Settings → Integrations → Observability** and select **Weights & Biases**.
2. Enter your API key. If you use a dedicated or self-managed W&B deployment, also enter its base URL.
3. Save.

## Notes

- A project can use its own credentials instead of the organization's.
