---
title: "IDE providers"
description: "Connect your JupyterHub to GGX so users can work in managed Jupyter notebooks."
---

IDE providers give users a development environment to open from GGX. Configure them in **Org Settings → Integrations → IDE providers**.

| Integration | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [JupyterHub](jupyterhub/) | Open managed Jupyter notebooks from your own JupyterHub. |
